Welcome to Pointless Wars, the single-player and multiplayer game that takes strategy and skill to defeat your opponent in a pointless war! In this game, players compete to destroy their opponent's castle by buying units to attack, upgrading their castle to earn more gold, and even summoning meteors to defend. Pointless Wars has something for everyone with multiple modes to select from such as single-player against the computer, two-player for a quick friendly match, or even make your tournament with three up to eight players. Use your brain against your opponent in this puzzler of a party game.
